Message from the President

Welcome to NICE-SA FUTA. Our members are training to build the roads, bridges, and water systems that Nigeria depends on. This session, we are focused on practical skills, mentorship from practicing engineers, and a community where every student has a place.

I invite you to attend our programs, take on a role, and help us leave the association stronger than we found it.

Engr. John DoePresident, NICE-SA FUTA
