Our history

NICE-SA FUTA is the student arm of the Nigerian Institution of Civil Engineers at the Federal University of Technology, Akure. It was founded in 2014 by the first set of civil engineering students who wanted a platform to learn beyond the classroom and connect with practising engineers.

From those early meetings, the association grew into a community with regular programs, tutoring, site visits and mentorship. Each executive has built on the work of the last, shaping students into skilled and ethical professionals.

Today, NICE-SA FUTA continues to bridge the gap between academic study and the demands of the engineering profession.
